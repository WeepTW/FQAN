# Questionnaire for financial narrative interfaces

This bilingual questionnaire evaluates whether a financial narrative visualization helps participants understand a chart, locate numerical evidence, and judge consistency between a generated statement and displayed data. It measures an explanatory interface, not investment intent or trading behavior.

## Structure

| Component | Items | Construct | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Q1-Q3 | Chart-reading, generative-AI, and source-checking experience | Frequency |
| Objective performance | Q4-Q7 | Consistency, comprehension, numerical identification, and evidence location | Correct or incorrect |
| Subjective perception | Q8-Q10 | Information comprehension | 5-point Likert scale |
| Subjective perception | Q11-Q13 | Evidence traceability | 5-point Likert scale |

The instrument does not claim to measure trust, usability, cognitive load, or investment quality.

## Use

Open `index.html` for English or `index_zh.html` for Traditional Chinese. Keep participant responses outside the repository, use study identifiers instead of names, and follow the approved consent and retention procedure. Remove document properties and local paths before sharing exports.
